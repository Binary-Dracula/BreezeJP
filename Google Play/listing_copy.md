BreezeJP — The Ultimate Offline Japanese News Learning Tool built for efficiency and focus.

If you are looking for a platform that harmonizes deep reading, natural listening, and systematic review, BreezeJP is your best companion. We don't just bridge you to news; we reconstruct your learning path through cutting-edge technology.

**🌟 Core Feature Matrix:**

**1. Deeply Tailored NHK Easy News**
- **Daily Synchronization**: Automatically fetch the latest NHK Easy News to keep your study materials fresh.
- **Structured Layout**: Optimized content arrangement for an immersive reading experience on mobile screens.
- **Contextual Search**: Tap any word for deep parsing, meaning, and usage examples.

**2. Industry-Leading "Precision Furigana Engine"**
- **Accurate Annotations**: Powered by precise morphological analysis (UniDic), strictly adhering to official Okurigana rules.
- **Flexible Display**: Toggles for full ruby icons, kanji only, or tap-to-show. Adjust the difficulty based on your proficiency.
- **Sense-Aware**: Accurately distinguishes the correct pronunciation of polyphonic kanji based on context.

**3. Neural-Driven "Offline High-Fidelity Voice" (Voicevox TTS)**
- **Human-Like Quality**: Built-in high-performance neural TTS engine providing natural intonation and pitch accent.
- **100% Offline**: Once the voice model is downloaded, all listening training is performed locally—no data usage, no latency, zero privacy leaks.
- **Background Play**: Master listening skills during your commute with our optimized background audio player.

**4. Comprehensive Vocabulary & Grammar Support**
- **Built-in Grammar Library**: Curated core points from N5 to N1, explored through real-world news examples.
- **Kana Mastery**: Complete module for Hiragana and Katakana with stroke order demonstrations.
- **Smart Notebook**: Add words directly from articles with their original contextual sentences.

**5. Systematic Memory & Review**
- **Multiple Test Types**: Consolidate your knowledge via Spelling quizzes, multiple-choice questions, and more.
- **Spaced Repetition Logic**: Optimized review schedules to ensure efficient long-term retention.

**6. Visualized Learning Statistics**
- **Data-Driven Progress**: Beautiful charts displaying your reading counts, listening hours, and vocabulary growth.
- **Achievement Tracking**: Record your daily consistency with a dedicated study calendar.

**💎 Our Technical Philosophy:**
- **Privacy First**: All processing and voice synthesis stay on your device. Your data remains yours.
- **Extreme Purity**: **Zero Ads**. We believe learning should be free of noise and commercial distractions.
- **Offline First**: Your portable learning companion that works anytime, anywhere.
